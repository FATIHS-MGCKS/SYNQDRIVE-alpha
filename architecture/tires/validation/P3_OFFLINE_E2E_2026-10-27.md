## 7. Scientific limitations

1. **Measured tread** — only where accepted measurement + odometer anchor; cohort has **NULL `odometer_at_measurement` on all accepted rows**.
2. **Model-estimated tread** — V3 intervals from prior + ledger; not validated against independent gauge remeasurement.
3. **Uncalibrated prior** — `BASELINE_ESTIMATE` / L1 rate with `RATE_INSUFFICIENT_POINTS`.
4. **Insufficient evidence** — verification layer correctly blocks treating estimates as measured.
5. **DS4 dynamic wear** — β=0; shadow inputs exist (Tesla/Tiguan) but **not applied** to customer wear.

## 8. Production activation blockers

1. Production deploy SHA **behind** `1cc5b91c` (P2 + #2201 safety not on VPS at audit time).
2. Cutover env flags **absent** on production `backend.env`.
3. Fleet evidence quality: **no measurement odometer**, Arteon **no tread measurements** — authoritative V3 would surface `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` / measurement-required rental posture fleet-wide for this cohort.
4. No end-to-end attestation of `TireHealthService.getSummary` + UI on production with `ENABLED=true`.
5. Independent field validation campaign for absolute mm accuracy **not in scope** (explicitly not claimed).

## 9. Verdict

**INCONCLUSIVE** for production GO: consumer chain and cutover mechanics validate on `main` with read-only replay, but real-vehicle evidence does not yet support replacing V2 as the single authoritative customer-facing wear presentation without a prolonged mixed-insufficient state. Recommend measurement/odometer remediation on fleet cohort before `ENABLED=true` / `AUTHORITATIVE=true`.
